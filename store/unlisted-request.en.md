## What is the app for?

EAD is the field app of Les Entreprises Allen Dumaresq, a civil-engineering contractor in Quebec, Canada. The company's office, foremen and site workers use it on their phones to open the construction plans of their projects on site (with or without a network), read project documents, take site photos and write the daily report of each site. It is the iPhone version of the company's own web app, https://ead-chantier.vercel.app.

## Who will use it?

Only the employees of Les Entreprises Allen Dumaresq: the office staff, the foremen and the site workers. About `<NUMBER_OF_EMPLOYEES>` people.

## Why unlisted?

The app is of no use to the public: it shows nothing without an account, and accounts are given by the company to its own employees only (a personal link, the QR code of a site, or a code given by the office). Listing it publicly would only bring people who cannot sign in. Unlisted distribution lets the office send one link to each new employee, who installs the app from the App Store like any other app.

## Why not Apple Business Manager (custom apps)?

The staff use their own iPhones, not phones owned or managed by the company. Custom apps through Apple Business Manager reach devices that the company manages, or people through redemption codes that the office would have to obtain, track and hand out one by one, for every new employee and every replaced phone. Most of the site workers are not comfortable with software: a single App Store link, sent by text message with their invitation, is what works for them.
