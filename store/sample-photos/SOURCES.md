# Sample site photos: sources

Photographs used by `scripts/store-sample.mts` as the sample project's site photos in the App Store screenshots. None shows an EAD site. Each one is in the public domain (Public Domain Mark 1.0, US government works) or dedicated to it (CC0 1.0), so it may be used without attribution, commercially included; the source is noted anyway. They were found through Openverse, resized to 1024 px wide and saved without their metadata. The script marks each one "EXEMPLE" and gives it a caption that ends with "(exemple)".
